Our Story

Where Family Recipes Meet Fine Dining

Founded in 2018, Amahoro Kitchen — "Kitchen of Peace" — began as a family table of eight seats. Today we welcome guests from all over the world, but the philosophy never changed: honest Rwandan ingredients, cooked with fire and patience.

Our kitchen works directly with smallholder farms across the Southern and Northern provinces, and our dining room glows with the work of local artisans.
