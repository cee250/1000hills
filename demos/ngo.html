Our Mission

Locally Led. Community Driven.

Hope Rwanda Foundation was founded by Rwandans, for Rwandans. We don't impose solutions — we listen to communities and fund what they tell us matters most.

What We Do

Four Programs, One Goal

Integrated support that follows a child from classroom to career.

Education Support

Fees, materials and tutoring for vulnerable pupils — from primary to university.

Health & Nutrition

School feeding programs and family health education in partner schools.

Clean Water

Boreholes, rainwater systems and maintenance training for villages.

Skills Training

Tailoring, carpentry and digital skills for out-of-school youth.
